# Acme Corp Information Security Policy (Sample)

This is a fictional sample policy for demonstration. It contains no real company data.

## 1. Access Management

User accounts must be approved by a manager before they are created. Accounts shall be reviewed every quarter and disabled when no longer needed.
All employees must use multi-factor authentication to access company systems.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and must not be reused.
Staff shall be given only the access required to do their job.
Remote access to the network must go through the company VPN.

## 2. Incident Response

The security team shall maintain an incident response plan and test it at least once a year.
All employees must report suspected security incidents to the security team within 24 hours.
The company will notify affected customers and regulators of a breach as required by law.

## 3. Data Protection

Customer data must be encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
Sensitive data stored on laptops and servers shall be encrypted at rest.
Backups of critical systems must be taken daily and stored offsite.
Retired hard drives must be physically destroyed before disposal.

## 4. Vulnerability and Change Management

Critical security patches must be applied within 14 days of release.
Vulnerability scans shall be run monthly on all production systems.
All changes to production systems must be approved through a documented change request.

## 5. Workforce

New hires must complete security awareness training within 30 days and annually afterward.
Background checks are required for all employees before they receive system access.
Access must be revoked on the employee's last day.

## 6. Third Parties

Vendors that handle company data must be assessed for security before a contract is signed.
